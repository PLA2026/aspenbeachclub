Membership

Anyone may apply.
Very few are admitted.

Aspen Beach Club welcomes anyone to apply for membership but is extremely discerning with whom we admit. Tell us why you would be a good addition to the Club, and the Membership Committee will review it prior to making our decision.

Good luck.

Application for Membership

The Committee requires a name and a valid email.

Club membership is complimentary. NAMASTAY membership, if selected, is billed monthly upon admission. By applying, you agree to receive correspondence from the Club.

The Committee does not announce its timeline. Correspondence will follow by email.

On-Site Designer

Toasted Bees

The Club's print, patch, and stitch studio, made available to visitors.

Toasted Bees designs and builds swag collections for the occasions guests plan around the Club. Bring the date, the guest list, and the idea. Toasted Bees returns with a collection made for it: apparel, patches, hats, and keepsakes, each one marked for the day.

Occasions

  • Birthday parties
  • Weddings
  • Bar and bat mitzvahs
  • Anniversaries
  • Reunions
  • Team outings

How it works

  1. Consult. Request a consultation through the Concierge and tell us about the occasion.
  2. Design. Toasted Bees drafts a crest, a collection, and the pieces to carry it.
  3. Build. Every piece is printed, patched, or stitched, then delivered before the day.
