Safe for everyone in the room.

Private sessions happen in private spaces, so trust runs both ways. Here is how we protect our clients and our instructors.

Verified clients

Every client verifies a government-issued photo ID before their first session. Hotel bookings must match the registered guest.

Vetted instructors

Every instructor is certified, background-checked and insured before teaching a single session.

Check-in on every visit

Instructors check in on arrival and out on departure, with a direct line to our safety team the whole time.
